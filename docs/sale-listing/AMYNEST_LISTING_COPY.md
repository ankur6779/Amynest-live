## D. 300-word detailed listing

**PUBLIC-SAFE** (~300 words)

AmyNest AI is a live, multi-platform parenting application. Families use it on the web (www.amynest.in), on Google Play as “AmyNest AI: Smart Parenting” (package com.amynest.app, production release 106 / 1.4.63 as of 2 October 2026 Play Console inspection), and on the Apple App Store (App ID 6767664343). The product is built as a React web app with an Express API, PostgreSQL, and Redis, delivered through Cloudflare and a Hetzner/Coolify origin. Android ships as a WebView wrapper; iOS ships as a Capacitor shell.

What a parent actually gets is a daily hub plus tools: child profiles, routine generation, Ask Amy, learning (including phonics and study), speech coach, Health Lab, games, stories and creativity tiles (worksheets, coloring books, art/craft), nutrition, audio lessons, and infant-oriented hub components. Some areas are premium-gated. Not every module has been end-to-end recertified in the latest diligence pass; code presence is not a usage guarantee.

The commercial seller is AmyWorld, the sole proprietorship of Ankur Raman. The domain amynest.in and the Cloudflare account are personally held and are intended to transfer with the deal. Indian Patent Application No. 202611059355 (filed 10 May 2026; title concerning adaptive child-development routine generation) is included. The patent is pending. It is not granted.

Revenue is early-stage. RevenueCat (24 Sep 2026): 3 active paid, 0 trials, USD 5 MRR, USD 26.14 lifetime gross, USD 16.83 lifetime proceeds. Google Play verified gross charges May–August 2026: ₹796 (four monthly charges of ₹199; duplicate May file excluded; September Play report not verified). Apple: one verified proceeds amount ₹1,080.31 (June 2026 report). Do not sum these as a single net number. Google Ads conversions are installs/first opens, not purchasers. A Google Ads campaign remains enabled; 90-day spend of INR 18,946.27 is verified and is larger than verified subscription cash.

The sale is an asset transaction. Legal title documentation, signed agreements, patent assignment, and vendor account transfers complete at closing. App-store transfers require the buyer’s Play and Apple developer accounts.
