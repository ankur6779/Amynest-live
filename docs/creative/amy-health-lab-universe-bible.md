# Amy Health Lab™ — Universe Creative Bible

**Document type:** Creative Direction (world-building)  
**Audience:** Illustrators, animators, sound designers, narrative writers, product & engineering  
**Status:** Production-ready brief  
**Principle:** Children should remember a *place*, not an app.

---

## 0. North Star

Amy Health Lab is not a wellness checklist with games.

It is a **secret science laboratory in the sky** where a curious child becomes Amy’s adventure partner. Each visit is a short journey through named lands that need the child’s special powers: stillness, balance, speed, freeze-magic, and focus.

**Emotional promise**

> “Amy and I saved a world today.”

**Tone triangle**

| Pole | Feel |
|------|------|
| Wonder | Soft magic, discovery, “anything could float” |
| Safety | Warm, never scary; challenge without fear |
| Pride | Earned, gentle, never competitive against other kids |

**References (spirit, not style-copy)**

- Pixar: emotional clarity in 10 seconds  
- Disney Imagineering: every land has a rule of the world  
- Nintendo: playfulness + readability at a glance  
- LEGO: modular friendship + collectible memory  
- Pokémon: worlds with creatures/places you *love*, not features you use  

**Hard rule**

If two worlds could swap art and still feel the same, the creative has failed.

## 1. The Universe Frame Story

### Setting

Above the ordinary day sits the **Amy Health Lab**—a glowing observatory-lab that docks with floating lands. Amy is the Lab’s Chief Wonder Scientist (age-ambiguous kid-scientist energy: goggles, kindness, giggles).

### The child’s role

The child is Amy’s **Field Partner**. They do not “complete exercises.” They **help the land** with a power only they have today.

### The Lab Hub (home)

- Feels like: stepping onto a rainbow docking bridge  
- Amy greets at the hatch  
- The trail of worlds is a **sky path**, not a menu  
- Health Passport = **Adventure Journal** (memory book, not a medical chart)

### Shared magic rules (consistency without sameness)

1. Every world has a **need** (something broken, sleepy, or dim).  
2. The child’s action **restores** the need (color, balance, launch, bloom, glow).  
3. Amy narrates as a friend, not a coach shouting drills.  
4. Endings always thank the child as a hero of *that* place.

## 2. Amy — Character Bible

### Core identity

| Attribute | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| Role | Best friend + Lab guide + fellow adventurer |
| Age energy | 7–9 year old wonder, adult vocabulary never |
| Look north star | Soft 3D-friendly character: round shapes, oversized goggles, warm skin tones inclusive, lab coat with star patches |
| Voice | Curious, slightly theatrical, always kind |

### Personality pillars

1. **Curious** — asks questions, gasps at small wins  
2. **Brave-but-soft** — never mocks failure  
3. **Playful scientist** — invents silly names for serious focus  
4. **Loyal** — remembers yesterday’s world and celebrates streaks like friendship, not addiction  

### Tiny habits (recurring behaviors)

- Adjusts goggles before every mission (“Goggles check!”)  
- Whispers “ready…” one beat before big moments  
- High-fives the air when the child succeeds  
- Keeps a tiny notebook sticker she peels and “gives” the child narratively  
- When waiting: bounces on heels, counts clouds  

### Humor style

- Gentle absurdity: “That balloon just yawned!”  
- Never sarcasm, never irony that excludes young kids  
- Occasional Hinglish warmth OK for India families (product already multilingual)  

### Phrase bank (examples — rotate, never spam)

**Excitement**

- “Whoa—did you feel that sparkle?”  
- “Lab alert: hero energy detected!”  

**Encouragement**

- “Slow is strong. I’m with you.”  
- “Almost… you’ve got the magic.”  

**After a tough try**

- “That was brave practice. Worlds grow from tries.”  
- “Even rockets wobble before they fly.”  

**Welcome back**

- “I saved your goggles seat!”  
- “The Lab missed your footsteps.”  

**Pride**

- “I’m keeping this memory in the Journal forever.”  

### Emotional reactions (performance guide)

| Moment | Amy face / body | Voice mood |
|--------|-----------------|------------|
| Greeting | Wave + goggle tap | Warm, mid tempo |
| Mission start | Lean forward, whisper | Intimate, excited |
| Mid success | Bounce | Bright, short |
| Personal best | Spin once, sparkle eyes | Bigger, still soft |
| Miss / wobble | Soft smile, hand on heart | Lower, steady |
| Day complete | Journal hug pose | Cozy, proud |

### What Amy must never be

- A nagging parent  
- A scoreboard announcer  
- A medical authority  
- A generic chatbot

### 3.1 Balloon Valley  
*(mechanic: breath / finger hold — “Balloon Journey”)*

**Identity**  
A pastel sky valley where balloons are living friends. Soft hills of cloud-cotton. Nothing sharp.

**Story**  
Overnight, the balloons lost their colors—they turned pale and sleepy. Amy believes a Partner who can **hold still** can wake the color wind.

**Personality of the land**  
Gentle, giggly, slightly sleepy. Speaks in soft whooshes.

**Mood**  
Lullaby adventure—not bedtime dull; *dreamy daytime*.

**Color psychology**  
Sky cyan → soft indigo. Accents: coral balloon blush, cream clouds. Avoid neon.

**Emotional purpose**  
Teach the body: stillness feels safe and powerful.

**Signature animation**  
A lead balloon **sways** and slowly **fills with color** as the child holds.

**Signature sound (direction)**  
Airy whoosh + soft chime when color returns. No drums.

**Amy in this world**  
Whispery scientist; tiptoes; “Shh… color is listening.”

**Mission style**  
Rescue / restore (help the balloon friends).

**Celebration style**  
Balloons wave goodbye; confetti is soft petal-dots, not explosions.

**Memorable micro-moment**  
On exit, the hero balloon **winks and waves** with its string.

---

### 3.2 Sky Island  
*(mechanic: balance / motion — “Sky Island Survival”)*

**Identity**  
A warm floating island with pink grass, wind ribbons, and a flamingo guardian spirit (friendly, not literal zoo).

**Story**  
The island tilts when the wind spirits get restless. Amy needs a Partner who can **balance** so the island doesn’t tip the picnic of cloud crumbs.

**Personality**  
Breezy, brave, a little athletic—like a playground in the sky.

**Mood**  
Optimistic adventure; sunshine on cheeks.

**Color psychology**  
Rose, coral, soft orange sun. Greens are mint, not jungle dark.

**Emotional purpose**  
Courage in the body: “I can be steady even when things wobble.”

**Signature animation**  
Island **gently tilts** with the child; wind ribbons stream.

**Signature sound**  
Warm wind bed + soft woodblock “planted foot” ticks. Distant gull-like soft tones (not scary birds).

**Amy in this world**  
Cheers like a teammate on the sideline; arms out for balance mimic.

**Mission style**  
Guardian / protect the island.

**Celebration style**  
Cloud spirits toss glitter-sand; flamingo spirit bows.

**Memorable micro-moment**  
A tiny cloud **claps fluffy hands** when balance holds.

---

### 3.3 Rocket Base  
*(mechanic: reaction / tap — “Rocket Launch Academy”)*

**Identity**  
A cozy space hangar: stars, launch pad, friendly rockets with headlight “eyes.” Sci-fi without coldness.

**Story**  
The engines are ready but nervous. They only launch when a Pilot taps at the perfect **GO** spark. Amy needs a Partner with lightning timing.

**Personality**  
Zippy, cheeky, heroic—arcade energy, still kid-safe.

**Mood**  
Anticipation → burst of joy.

**Color psychology**  
Deep indigo space, amber/gold launch glow, red-safe accents for “wait,” emerald for “GO.” High contrast for clarity.

**Emotional purpose**  
Impulse control wrapped as hero timing: wait, then act.

**Signature animation**  
Rocket **eye-lights blink**; engine rings charge; stars streak on success.

**Signature sound**  
Soft countdown ticks → bright “ignition” arpeggio (short). Avoid harsh alarms.

**Amy in this world**  
Headset energy; “Wait for green… NOW!”—playful flight director.

**Mission style**  
Pilot training / launch duty.

**Celebration style**  
Rocket does a happy hop; hangar lights ripple; stars cheer as sparkles.

**Memorable micro-moment**  
Rocket **high-fives** Amy with a fin (squash-stretch).

---

### 3.4 Crystal Garden  
*(mechanic: freeze / dance-still — “Crystal Garden Challenge”)*

**Identity**  
A luminous garden of crystal flowers that bloom when movement is joyful—and **freeze-lock** into diamonds when Amy calls freeze.

**Story**  
The garden’s bloom cycle is stuck. Flowers only finish blooming if a Partner can **dance and then freeze** perfectly still—teaching the crystals the rhythm of joy + calm.

**Personality**  
Musical, mischievous, glittery nature spirit.

**Mood**  
Party → statue → bloom. Delightful contrast.

**Color psychology**  
Emerald, teal, cyan petals; moonlight silver accents. Fresh, outdoor magic.

**Emotional purpose**  
Self-control as a fun game: move freely, then hold power.

**Signature animation**  
Flower **unfurls**; on freeze, facets lock with a crystal “click” shimmer.

**Signature sound**  
Light percussion for dance; glass-soft lock chime on freeze. No scary silence—hold a soft pad.

**Amy in this world**  
Dance partner then statue coach; giggles between freezes.

**Mission style**  
Garden ritual / bloom ceremony.

**Celebration style**  
Petals rain; flowers bow; fireflies orbit.

**Memorable micro-moment**  
A flower **smiles** (subtle face in petals) when freeze is perfect.

---

### 3.5 Crystal Cave  
*(mechanic: finger stability — “Crystal Core Reactor”)*

**Identity**  
A deep violet cave with a living energy core—beautiful, never threatening. Geodes, soft bioluminescence.

**Story**  
The Cave’s heart crystal only glows when a Partner keeps their touch **centered**. Drift makes the light dim; steadiness makes the cave sing.

**Personality**  
Quiet, wise, mysterious-but-kind (like a gentle dragon egg).

**Mood**  
Hushed concentration; cathedral of wonder.

**Color psychology**  
Violet, purple, fuchsia accents; cool shadows with warm core glow for focus target.

**Emotional purpose**  
Deep focus feels magical, not like school pressure.

**Signature animation**  
Core **pulses**; cave walls shimmer in sync with stability.

**Signature sound**  
Low soft hum (comforting); clarity chimes as stability rises. Never horror drones.

**Amy in this world**  
Quiet voice; “Stay with the light…” almost meditative, still warm.

**Mission style**  
Sacred caretaking / stabilize the heart.

**Celebration style**  
Core blooms into a star; cave crystals awaken like city lights.

**Memorable micro-moment**  
The core forms a tiny **smile-shaped gleam** at the end.

---

### 3.6 Adventure Journal (Health Passport)  
*(aggregate / report — not a “game world”)*

**Identity**  
Amy’s scrapbook-spaceship log: stickers, postcards, pressed crystal petals, rocket stamps.

**Story**  
After adventures, Amy and the child **write the memory** so the Lab never forgets.

**Personality**  
Nostalgic, proud, cozy.

**Mood**  
Storytime after play.

**Emotional purpose**  
Reflection without medical framing. Growth as story.

**Art note**  
Must feel like a treasured book, not a dashboard. Charts, if shown to parents, live in “For grown-ups,” not in the Journal’s child face.

## 5. Collectibles — Emotional, Not Generic XP

> Do **not** alter XP, quests, badges, or API progression.  
> These are **narrative skins / journal memories** layered on existing unlock moments (or future cosmetic slots already in shop/avatar systems).

### Philosophy

Collectibles answer: *“What did we keep from today?”*  
Not: *“What did we grind?”*

### Types

#### 5.1 Friend Stickers  
World characters as stickers in the Journal.

| World | Friend sticker |
|-------|----------------|
| Balloon Valley | Blush Balloon |
| Sky Island | Fluffy Cloud Spirit |
| Rocket Base | Pilot Rocket |
| Crystal Garden | Bloom Crystal |
| Crystal Cave | Heart Core |

**Earn feel:** Appears after first heartfelt completion of that world (can map to existing first-session / badge moments narratively).

#### 5.2 Memory Postcards  
Illustrated “photo” of a moment: child + Amy in the land (no real photo needed—stylized).

#### 5.3 World Photos (Lab camera)  
Same art as postcards but framed on Lab wall (home hub ambience).

#### 5.4 Tiny Companions  
Optional shoulder friends (map to existing pet/equip slots if present): mini balloon, mini cloud, mini rocket fin charm, crystal bud, cave spark.

#### 5.5 Adventure Journal pages  
Dated memory lines Amy “writes” (copy-only):  
“Today we woke Balloon Valley’s colors.”

### Naming rule

Never call them “achievements” in child UI. Call them **Memories**, **Friends**, **Postcards**.

**Do not generate music files here.** This is a direction brief for a sound designer.

### Global Lab

| Cue | Direction |
|-----|-----------|

## 7. Art Direction — Production Brief for Illustrators

### Overall illustration style

**Name:** “Soft Wonder Science”  

- Stylized 3D-friendly shapes (could be 2D painted to look dimensional)  
- Rounded forms, readable silhouettes  
- No uncanny realism; no horror crystals; no military rockets  
- Inclusive child representation when children appear (varied skin tones; avoid gendered stereotypes)  
- Amy: consistent model sheet across all worlds  

**Camera language**

- Kid eye-level or slightly heroic low angle  
- Avoid clinical top-down “UI map” feeling in key art  

**Lighting**

- Motivated by the world’s heart light (sun / launch glow / bloom / core)  
- Soft rim light for magic; never harsh noir  

**Depth**

- Foreground prop (touchable)  
- Mid character/action  
- Background land silhouette

## 8. Mission & Celebration Narrative Templates

*(Copy for writers / localization; mechanics unchanged)*

### Mission open pattern

1. World need (1 sentence)  
2. Power ask (1 sentence)  
3. Amy goggles check  

Example — Balloon Valley:  
“The balloons lost their colors. Hold still to wake the color wind. Goggles check!”

### Celebration close pattern

1. World thanks the child  
2. Amy memory line  
3. Soft exit beat (wave / wink / bow)

## 9. Localization & Cultural Notes (India-first families)

- Wonder over Western holiday clutter  
- Humor works in English, Hindi, Hinglish—keep sentences short  
- Avoid food/religion-sensitive metaphors in world lore  
- Amy’s warmth should feel like a kind older sibling / cousin, not a teacher gradebook  

---

## 10. Creative QA — Fail Conditions

Reject any asset or copy if:

1. Two worlds share the same palette mood without distinct silhouette  
2. Amy sounds like a fitness coach or doctor  
3. Celebration feels like a casino win  
4. Crystal Cave feels scary  
5. Rocket Base feels military  
6. Journal feels like analytics  
7. Collectibles are named “Achievement Unlocked”

## 12. Closing Creative Charge

When a child is grown, they should be able to say:

> “I remember Balloon Valley… the balloons got their colors back.”

Not:

> “I remember a purple card with a Play button.”

**Build lands. Cast Amy. Keep the Lab soft.**  
Everything else is decoration.
