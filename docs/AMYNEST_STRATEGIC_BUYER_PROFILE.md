# AmyNest — Strategic Buyer Profile

**Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Purpose:** Who could rationally pay $100,000 when a MicroSaaS buyer will not.  
**Not a list of named companies in process.** Categories only. No claim that any firm is in diligence.

Live AmyNest economics remain: **$5 MRR, 3 paid, $26.14 lifetime RC gross.** Strategic premium must come from **the buyer’s assets**, not AmyNest’s.

---

## The “one buyer” theory

**Who AmyNest is worth $100K to:**

A **parenting, family, or Indian edtech company that already has distribution** (app installs, school/parent community, or a large content audience) and **does not have** a production multi-module parent OS with live IAP on both stores.

**What they are buying:** 4–8 months of store+billing+domain-logic tuition and a killable module catalog they can feed **their** users into.

**What they are not buying:** cash flow, a growth engine, a brand, a model, or 12,000 families (that claim is contradicted by RC).

**Why $100k can be cheap *for them*:** if their product team is 3 people at $15k/month fully loaded, six months is **$90k of payroll** before store delay. $100k is a **project substitution**. If they do **not** have that payroll burn, $100k is expensive.

## What the one buyer must believe (checklist)

1. “We will put **our** users into this, not scale AmyNest UA.”  
2. “Production IAP on both stores + speech/safety is on our critical path this half.”  
3. “$100k < internal build + delay.”  
4. “We can delete 50% of modules on day 1.”  
5. “Patent and 12,000-family claims will be stripped before we put our brand on it.”  
6. “Founder will handover for 30 days.”

If any of 1–3 is false, this buyer should bid **$25k–$45k** or build.
