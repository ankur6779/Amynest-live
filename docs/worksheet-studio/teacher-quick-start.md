# Worksheet Studio — Teacher Quick Start

## Create a worksheet (30 seconds)

1. Open **Worksheet Studio** from the app menu
2. Pick a **template** or describe your topic
3. Choose **class**, **subject**, **difficulty**, and **pages**
4. Tap **Generate Worksheet**

## Organize your work

- **Library** — search, favorite, folder, export, reopen
- **Productivity** — weekly plan (Mon–Fri), homework pack, classroom pack, bulk generate
- **Branding** — set your school logo, colors, footer (applies to all exports automatically)

## Edit on mobile

- Tap text to edit; drag objects with one finger
- Swipe left/right to change pages
- Pinch to zoom; use the toolbar for text, images, shapes
- **Export** → PDF, DOCX, PNG, print, or share

## Language

Choose **English**, **Hindi**, or **Bilingual** on the home screen before generating.

## Offline

Worksheets generate and save locally without internet. AI features use a local template when offline.

## Tips

- Resume your last draft from the home screen
- Version history is in the autosave indicator (cloud icon)
- Answer keys: generate with **Generate Answer Key** or from the editor AI assistant
