**Evidence:** Jul 4: 7 finish, 2 generate, only 3 users hit generate screen. Gap persists post-deploy.  
**Impact:** Converts acquisition spike (22 first opens) into activated users; highest retention lever.  
**ROI:** Every +1 routine user ≈ measurable D1 cohort improvement.

### 2. D1 push for Jul 4 cohort with `finish_clicked` but no `routine_generated` (P0)
**Evidence:** ~5 users finished onboarding without generating Jul 4; 22 new installs same day.  
**Impact:** Same-day activation before D1 drop (D1 still 6.8%).  
**ROI:** Low cost; targets proven leak with named segment.

### 3. Fix `device_header_missing` noise (P1 measurement)
**Evidence:** 498 events / 24 users Jul 4 (~21 per user); obscures funnel dashboards.  
**Impact:** Cleaner production analytics for remaining 12 days of analyst program.  
**ROI:** Unblocks accurate daily comparison — not user-facing but enables all other decisions.

### 4. Protect cancel-retention flow (P1 revenue)
**Evidence:** Jul 4: 1 trialing user `cancel_started` → `cancel_agent_retained` (saved).  
**Impact:** Direct MRR protection; 21 trialing users at risk.  
**ROI:** One save > marginal feature work; do not regress cancel agent.

### 5. Verify Phase 5 activation instrumentation in staging smoke test (P1)
**Evidence:** `paywall_deferred_activation`, `first_routine_generated`, `activation_resume` all **0** on Jul 4 despite deploy.
