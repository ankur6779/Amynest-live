# AmyNest — 30-Day Pre-Sale Plan

**Date:** 14 September 2026  
**Goal:** Raise **buyer confidence and close probability**, not ship features.  
**Non-goals:** New modules, more UA spend, “look bigger” install spikes.  
**Constraint:** This document is a plan. It does **not** implement anything.

Impact = effect on sale price or probability. Buyer value = what an IC actually opens.

---

The $100k gap is **not** a missing widget. It is **trust + title + a strategic story**. Thirty days cannot produce 1,000 paid users. Thirty days **can** stop a diligence walk over “12,000 families” and an unfiled patent.

## What this 30 days will **not** do

- It will not make $5 MRR into $2,000 MRR.  
- It will not make $100k a **financial** multiple.  
- It will not fix 0.04% conversion (that is Path B, 3–6 months, after UA is off).

It **can** move $100k probability from “~5% and dies in week 1 of DD” to “~10–20% with the right letter in the right inbox.”
