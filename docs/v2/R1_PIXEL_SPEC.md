# R1 Pixel Spec — Opening Experience

**Release:** R1  
**Status:** APPROVED FOR MANUFACTURE  
**Form:** Apple-grade production pixel specification  
**Not:** wireframe · React · CSS · components · HTML · Tailwind

---

## Locked Promise

AmyNest helps parents know  
their child's  
**next right thing**  
**today.**

R1 does not deliver the next right thing yet.  
R1 manufactures the emotional doorway that makes the promise believable.

---

## R1 Product Job

Convert **Install → Open → Continue**  
through calm, premium arrival.

R1 must never ask for signup.  
R1 must never sell premium.  
R1 must never show a feature mall.

---

# 1. Frame Geometry

### Stage
Full-bleed vertical stage.  
One continuous atmospheric field.  
No split panes. No header chrome. No footer chrome in the first arrival beat.

### Safe area
- Top safe inset honored for notch / status island  
- Bottom safe inset honored for home indicator  
- Left/right content never kisses device edges  
- Primary presence remains inside a centered optical column  

### Optical column
Center column occupies the middle third of the stage.  
All meaning lives here.  
Left and right thirds remain atmospheric negative space.

### One-handed ergonomics
- First 10 seconds require no reach into top corners  
- No critical action in extreme top-left / top-right  
- If a continue affordance appears after arrival, it lives in the lower natural thumb arc  
- Arrival itself is passive: look and breathe, not hunt and tap  

---

# 2. Visual Hierarchy

Strict rank order. Nothing may outrank a higher layer.

| Rank | Element | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Amy living light / presence | Emotional subject |
| 2 | Atmospheric depth field | Calm container |
| 3 | Optional arrival whisper line | Secondary language, after presence lands |
| 4 | Optional continue affordance | Appears only after calm is established |
| 5 | Everything else | Forbidden in R1 first beat |

### Forbidden on R1 stage
Feature icons · carousels · testimonials · pricing · login walls · social proof stacks · notification prompts · module previews · badge noise · countdown urgency

---

# 3. Eye Path

### Designed path
1. Enter center  
2. Rest on Amy light  
3. Absorb atmosphere  
4. Only then notice whisper language  
5. Only then notice continue  

### Eye-path law
If the eye leaves center before 3 seconds, hierarchy failed.

---

# 4. Amy Placement

### Position
Exact optical center, slightly above geometric midpoint  
(classic portrait gravity — presence sits where the face would sit in a poster).

### Scale
Large enough to feel like a companion in the room.  
Small enough that negative space still dominates.  
Amy is presence, not a sticker in a corner.

### Character treatment
Light-first Amy.  
Warm living core before merchandise detail.  
No hyperactive face looping.  
No mascot wave spam.

### Relationship to parent
Amy faces the parent emotionally, not the interface.  
Presence says: arrive.  
Not: tap me to open a menu.

---

# 5. Illustration Placement

### Allowed
- Atmospheric vapor / soft glow fields  
- Minimal abstract light geometry supporting Amy  
- Depth layers behind presence  

### Placement rules
- Illustration supports Amy; never competes  
- No secondary illustrated characters  
- No lifestyle collage strips  
- No “feature world” murals  

### Depth stack
1. Far dark void  
2. Mid atmospheric haze  
3. Near Amy light  
4. Optional foreground dust of light (very subtle)

---

# 6. Background Depth

### Depth recipe
- Deep ink base  
- Violet dusk mid-layer  
- Warm ivory/rose core light around Amy  
- Soft vignette edges  

### Depth feeling
Pre-dawn quiet home.  
Not outer-space gimmick.  
Not flat app-gray.  
Not candy gradient.

### Ambient light
Single soft key from above-front.  
No multi-color club lighting.  
No lens-flare spectacle.

---

# 7. Typography

### First-beat law
Almost no type in the first second.  
Presence before language.

### When language appears
Only after Amy light has settled.

### Hierarchy
1. **Whisper line** — one short arrival sentence  
2. Optional secondary calm clause — never a paragraph  
3. Continue label — utilitarian, quiet, late  

### Type psychology
Adult. Soft. Certain.  
No hype. No exclamation addiction.  
No “Welcome to the future of parenting!!!!”

### Microcopy psychology
Allowed tone:
- “You’re in the right place.”  
- “Let’s begin with your child.”  
- “Take a breath.”  

Forbidden tone:
- “Unlock 50+ features”  
- “Join millions”  
- “Start free trial now”  
- “Create account to continue”

R1 language invites continuation of calm — not conversion force.

---

# 8. Negative Space

### Ratio target
Majority of the board remains unused by content.  
Emptiness is the premium cue.

### Corner law
Top-left, top-right, bottom-left remain visually quiet in arrival.  
Bottom center/thumb arc may host late continue only.

### Clutter test
If a designer asks “where do we put the other thing?” — answer: nowhere in R1.

---

# 9. Motion Timing

Breath-speed only.

| Beat | Time | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0–300ms | Black/soft void hold | No motion yet |
| 300–1200ms | Ambient field breathes in | Slow luminance rise |
| 800–2200ms | Amy light emerges | Inhale rise + settle |
| 2200–3000ms | Hold | Stillness wins |
| 3000–4500ms | Whisper line fades in | Soft opacity, no slide-spam |
| 4500–6000ms | Continue affordance may appear | Late, quiet, grounded |

### Motion bans
Bounce · elastic overshoot · confetti · shimmer spam · looping excited character dances · aggressive parallax

### Transition timing out of R1
Exit must feel like a curtain parting into Trust (R2), not a hard route jump.  
Crossfade / soft deepen / light carries forward.  
Duration: calm 400–700ms emotional bridge.

---

# 10. Trust Signals

Trust is manufactured by refusal.

R1 proves trust by what it does **not** do:
- does not ask for account  
- does not ask for payment  
- does not threaten loss  
- does not overwhelm with choice  
- does not look like an ad  

Positive trust cues:
- composure  
- one subject  
- premium silence  
- Amy presence without demand  

---

# 11. CTA Psychology

### CTA role in R1
Optional, late, soft.  
Not the hero.  
Not a sales closer.

### CTA meaning
“Continue into calm understanding.”  
Not “Sign up.”  
Not “Start trial.”  
Not “See plans.”

### CTA appearance rule
Only after presence + whisper have landed.  
If CTA appears before 3 seconds, R1 is too hungry.

### CTA visual psychology
Quiet gravity in the thumb zone.  
Secondary to Amy.  
Never brighter than the living light.

---

# 12. Premium Cues

Premium in R1 means:
- restraint  
- darkness with warm life  
- slow timing  
- one subject  
- material softness (vapor glass / velvet void)  
- typographic understatement  

Premium does **not** mean:
- gold badges  
- gloss stickers  
- “PRO” banners  
- glassmorphism circus  
- celebrity lifestyle photography  

---

# 13. Accessibility

### Vision
- Contrast between whisper text and atmosphere must remain readable  
- Critical meaning cannot depend on color alone  
- Amy presence must remain distinguishable if motion is reduced  

### Motion
- Respect reduced-motion preference: replace inhale animation with static settled presence + gentle fade of text  
- No seizure-risk flicker  
- No rapid strobing light  

### Motor
- Continue target must be comfortably large and thumb-reachable when present  
- No precision-only gestures required to leave Opening  

### Cognitive
- One idea at a time  
- No simultaneous asks  
- Language at plain adult clarity  

### Sound
- Optional ambient only  
- Never required for comprehension  
- Must remain soft and non-startling  

---

# 14. Emotional Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Required feeling |
|---|---|
| Entry | Noise falls away |
| Presence lands | Companionable calm |
| Whisper appears | Reassurance without hype |
| Continue appears | Willingness, not pressure |
| Exit to R2 | Trust doorway opens |

If any checkpoint feels like marketing, reset the board.

---

# 15. Exact Parent Thoughts by Time

### After 1 second
**“This is quiet.”**  
Not confused. Not sold. Just a sudden drop in noise.

### After 3 seconds
**“I can breathe here.”**  
Amy presence is felt. The product already feels different from chaotic parenting apps.

### After 5 seconds
**“This seems premium… and safe.”**  
Still no ask. Trust begins because nothing is grabbing.

### After 10 seconds
**“Okay… I’m ready to continue.”**  
Whisper language has landed. Continue feels optional and natural.

### After 30 seconds
**“I want to see what this is for my child.”**  
Curiosity is personal now. The parent is emotionally prepared for Trust / child recognition (R2).  
They are **not** thinking about plans, features, or accounts.

---

# 16. Conversion Checkpoint — R1

### Should they continue?
**Yes.**  
That is the only conversion that matters in R1.

### Should they sign up?
**No.**  
Signup in R1 is a product crime. Value has not been lived yet.

### Should they trust Amy?
**Begin to trust — not fully trust.**  
R1 earns composure-trust:  
“This product will not attack me.”  
Full child-specific trust belongs to R2.  
Usefulness-trust belongs to R3–R4.

### Why?
Because Opening’s job is to lower defense and create willingness.  
If R1 asks for commitment, it converts fear into bounce.  
If R1 stays calm, it converts install into an open heart.

---

# 17. R1 Pass / Fail Gate

Ship R1 only if all are true:

- [ ] One subject only: Amy living light  
- [ ] Majority negative space preserved  
- [ ] No signup / premium / feature mall  
- [ ] Calm felt by 3 seconds  
- [ ] Continue is late and quiet  
- [ ] Parent thought at 30s is curiosity about their child, not about pricing  
- [ ] Reduced-motion fallback remains beautiful  
- [ ] Thumb-zone ergonomics honored  
- [ ] Transition into R2 carries the same living light  

Fail any one → do not ship.
