**Mode:** Lived experience only. **No redesign. No implementation.**

**Path walked:** Landing → Front Door → Signup → Today → Mission → Coach → Ask Amy → Premium → For Child → Back to Today  
**Law:** [`FOUNDER_TRANSLATION_LAW_V2.md`](./FOUNDER_TRANSLATION_LAW_V2.md) · [`ROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md`](./ROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md) · [`FOUNDER_TRANSLATION_VALIDATION.md`](./FOUNDER_TRANSLATION_VALIDATION.md)

## Verdict in one breath

The Nest rooms that were translated feel like **rooms of one Home**.  
The first-time parent path still passes through a **second product** at Signup, and a **wizard corridor** at Front Door.  
AmyNest is not yet one home end-to-end. It is a Nest house with a neon Keep and an unfinished vestibule.

## Founder Verdict

### Would you ship AmyNest today?

# **NO**

### WHY

Because a first-time parent does not live only in the rooms that scored 80+.  
They live the **path**.  
The path still contains a **neon Keep** and a **Vestibule that interrogates**.  
Those two rooms decide trust before Today can earn it.  
Translated Nest rooms prove the Home is possible.  
They do not yet prove the Home is continuous.  
Shipping today would ship **a beautiful Nest with a second product at the lock** — and a tired parent remembers the lock.
