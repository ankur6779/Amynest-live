# Dimension findings

---

## 1. Humanity — 6.5

**What Apple would praise**  
- “You are not alone.” Help photography. Moments “ten minutes.” Guidance sacred sentence. Today Home decides; parent acts.  
- Child Discovery as film, not a form.

**What Apple would reject**  
- “When feelings are heavy” soft-enters the same assistant that can answer: *You've used your free messages. Upgrade to continue.*  
- Exhausted-parent emotional support presented as companionship, then gated as quota.  
- Infant “3 free baby questions” framing.

**Blocker:** Monetizing crisis adjacency destroys first-party humanity.

---

## 2. Craft — 7.0

**Praise**  
- FE material system is intentional and rare.  
- Living openings (Infant · Speech · Nutrition · Health Lab · Birth Sky welcome · Guidance · Moments · Grow · Ask Amy) show real craft discipline.  
- Today Home Apple final 1% closed Dashboard language and competing heroes.

**Reject**  
- Craft stops at the AppLink. Neon / HubModule / Astro / bookstore are unfinished other products.  
- Dual living/legacy flags mean craft is optional.

**Blocker:** Craft as façade is not Design Review craft.

---

## 3. Hierarchy — 6.5

**Praise**  
- Home: one NRT → Begin.  
- Living opens: one recommend → quiet paths.  
- Hub doors: four rooms, not twenty tiles (Rooms V1).

**Reject**  
- Help / Understand / Care still present peer destination catalogues beside manufactured exceptions.  
- Grow leave = six-SKU edtech after calm room.  
- Speech body / Nutrition tabs reintroduce second heroes under calm opens.

**Blocker:** Hierarchy collapses after the first deepen.

---

## 4. Navigation — 6.0

**Praise**  
- Hub Exit Panel: Back to Home / Continue / Another room — rare and correct.  
- Room back: All rooms.  
- Tab Home renamed from Dashboard (Today Home 1%).

**Reject**  
| Issue | Why Apple cares |
|---|---|
| Tab labels **Amy Coach** · **Parenting Hub** | Product SKUs, not places of life |
| Hub tab description “Articles, tools & support” | Mall language under sanctuary Home |
| AppLink leave without shared exit ritual | Feels like app-switching |
| Deep-link anchors `sr-only` + `aria-hidden` | Recovery theatre, not VO recovery |

**Blocker:** Navigation still describes a suite, not a home.

---

## 5. Typography — 6.0

**Praise**  
- Quicksand sanctuary titles; closed identity ladder; living opens kill PRO shout on quiet chrome.

**Reject**  
- Abacus PRO Zone · Unlock CTAs · Amy AI Assistant · Amy Astro display · neon game titles.  
- Living titles over-bold vs FE whisper (systemic volume drift).

**Blocker:** Type planets multiply after the lobby.

---

## 6. Photography — 5.5

**Praise**  
- R1 five-shot film is first-party grade.  
- Manufactured opens reuse `ROOM_HEROES` correctly.

**Reject**  
- Talking Amy, Speech neon, Discovery Worlds, Grow HubModule, `/assistant`, Curiosity abandon photography.  
- Birth Sky Astro invents a cosmic pack.

**Blocker:** Photography is optional after the second tap — Apple treats it as identity.

---

## 7. Materials — 5.0

**Praise**  
- Night sanctuary + warm sand glass + FE grain/glass/spill is coherent when present.

**Reject**  
- Three illegal nights: neon club · cosmic void · violet galaxy.  
- Light SaaS exits (Grow, Assistant, Curiosity).  
- lz-glass / emerald / Crown residue on deepen.

**Blocker:** Material dialect is not one product.

---

## 8. Motion — 5.5

**Praise**  
- FE settle · living reduced-motion · breath not bounce on sanctuary surfaces.

**Reject**  
- Confetti bursts (study, check-in, Health Lab, math, phonics, routines…).  
- `animate-bounce` / `.amy-bounce` / AmyIcon bounce on legacy paths.  
- RewardCelebrationModal alive behind Rooms kill-switch.

**Blocker:** Motion schizophrenia = category confusion (parenting home vs kids game platform).

---

## 9. Accessibility — 4.5

**Praise**  
- `prefers-reduced-motion` widely honored.  
- Some focus-visible rings; Today Home 48px targets + `aria-describedby`.  
- Infant Care contrast fix raised living hero readability.

**Reject**  
| Gap | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Dynamic Type suppressed | `text-size-adjust: 100%` on `.fe-shell`; fixed px ladder |
| Hub ARIA incomplete | No `aria-expanded` / `aria-current` / live regions on rooms shell |
| Living path state | Active paths via `data-active` without `aria-pressed`/`aria-selected` |
| Decorative vs informative | Ambient `alt=""` fine; deep-link stubs `aria-hidden` useless |
| Uneven a11y investment | Gaming a11y prefs richer than parenting spine |

**Blocker:** Apple Design Review treats Dynamic Type + focus choreography as non-optional.

---

## 10. Voice — 6.5

**Praise**  
- `PREMIUM_VOICE` invitation on living opens.  
- Philosophy forbids unlock/FOMO/guilt patterns (with tests).  
- Companion empty: “I'm here…”

**Reject**  
- LockedBlock mall: “Unlock with Premium.”  
- Assistant / Infant quota upgrade Zap.  
- Explore Free / PRO / Unlock still on Grow leave-paths.  
- Tab and Hub copy still product-speak.

**Blocker:** Two voices — sanctuary whisper and store shout.

---

## 11. Privacy — 7.0

**Praise**  
- `NSPrivacyTracking = false`; no ATT theatre.  
- Privacy page: parent-managed child profiles; no ads to children; COPPA-ish framing.  
- Permission strings purpose-specific (mic Speech · camera milestones).  
- Trust-before-permission gate on `/begin` / signup.

**Reject**  
- Generic “respond to permission prompts” hint — not purpose-tied in the moment.  
- Gate may prompt mic/push after first value, not only at Speech use — tension with policy language.  
- AI disclaimer is footer-grade, not emotionally owned on Help crisis entry.  
- No first-party analytics-consent narrative in main app (tracking=false helps Store; Design Review still wants clarity).

**Blocker:** Privacy paperwork ahead of privacy *feeling* on AI/crisis paths. Not the worst score — not ship-blocking alone.

---

## 12. Trust — 5.0

**Praise**  
- Soft open earns trust. Medical/reflective disclaimers exist in Birth Sky / Health / Nutrition / Infant.  
- Free spine policy exists on paper.

**Reject**  
- Emotional companionship → quota wall.  
- Calm Care open → Health Lab XP shop / Nutrition streak.  
- Moments calm → neon game.  
- Understand calm → Grow unlock.  
- Dual universes via flags = “which product did I get?”

**Blocker:** Trust acquired at the door is spent by minute three.

---

## 13. Continuity — 5.5

**Praise**

- Manufactured openings continue room light.

**Reject**  
- Blind logo test across destinations: **NO** (global + visual audits).  
- Hard AppLink cuts.  
- Moments/Guidance/Grow/Ask Amy exceptions vs Help/Care peer catalogues = inconsistent architecture.

**Blocker:** Continuity is a lobby, not a house.

---

## 14. Premium — 4.5

**Reject**  
- Talking Amy achievements · Discovery XP · Health Lab coins · Grow Unlock · Assistant Upgrade · Curiosity free-book scarcity · Legacy Hub XP.  
- Premium interruption after second tap remains (Hub Apple audits: Premium ~4.0).

**Blocker:** Apple first-party premium feels inevitable after value — not interruptive after chrome.

## Every blocker (ordered)

Apple Design Review would stop shipment for the following. Fixing craft on Home does not clear them.

### Blocker 1 — Not one application
**Evidence:** Talking Amy neon · Speech live/talk neon · Discovery Worlds XP · Grow HubModule unlock · Birth Sky Astro · `/assistant` mode desk · Curiosity bookstore.  
**HIG:** Consistency · Continuity · Materials · Photography.  
**Why fatal:** First-party products do not federate categories under one icon.

### Blocker 2 — Emotional / crisis path monetized
**Evidence:** Feelings path → `/assistant?companion=1` → free-message wall + Upgrade; `hub_emotional` FeatureGate history; Infant baby-question quota.  
**HIG:** Humanity · Trust · Voice · Privacy (trust feeling).  
**Why fatal:** Gating “when the day is hard” behind upgrade is not a Health/Journal-adjacent product.

### Blocker 3 — Accessibility incomplete for sanctuary claims
**Evidence:** Dynamic Type clamped; Hub missing expanded/current/live VO; living active states not exposed; uneven a11y bar.  
**HIG:** Accessibility.  
**Why fatal:** Apple treats a11y as craft quality, not backlog.

### Blocker 4 — Motion carnival alive in portfolio
**Evidence:** Confetti, bounce, reward modals on learning/health/legacy Hub.  
**HIG:** Motion · Consistency · Humanity.  
**Why fatal:** Category confusion — parenting sanctuary vs kids game OS.

### Blocker 5 — Premium interruption after calm open
**Evidence:** XP/shop/unlock/Explore Free/Zap on leave-paths; Pack 5 only cleaned Hub quiet slot.  
**HIG:** Premium · Trust · Voice.  
**Why fatal:** Calm open + hard sell = bait pattern Design Review rejects.

### Blocker 6 — Navigation still a product suite
**Evidence:** Amy Coach · Parenting Hub tab naming; “articles, tools & support”; AppLink app-switching.  
**HIG:** Navigation · Hierarchy · Voice.  
**Why fatal:** Tab bars name life places; AmyNest tabs still name products.

### Blocker 7 — Photography / materials optional after deepen
**Evidence:** Visual regression Critical list — neon/light/cosmic exits abandon R1 sanctuary.  
**HIG:** Photography · Materials · Continuity.  
**Why fatal:** Identity cannot be lobby-only.

### Blocker 8 — Dual universe via kill-switches
**Evidence:** Living OFF + Rooms V1 OFF restore mall/neon/legacy.  
**HIG:** Consistency · Trust.  
**Why fatal:** Shipping two products under one flag matrix is not a finished design system.

### Blocker 9 — Performance weight vs first-party lightness
**Evidence:** Voice controller mass · Hub megapage · 1700kb warning culture.  
**HIG:** Performance · Craft.  
**Why fatal alone:** Soft blocker — compounds with Consistency.

### Blocker 10 — Hierarchy collapse inside rooms
**Evidence:** Help/Care/Understand peer catalogues; Nutrition sticky journey; Speech body shelves.  
**HIG:** Hierarchy · Consistency.  
**Why fatal alone:** Soft blocker — compounds with Continuity.
