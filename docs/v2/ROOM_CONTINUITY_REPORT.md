# Room Continuity Report

**Mode:** Continuity walk only. No UI. No implementation. No redesign.  
**Authority:** [`ROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md`](./ROOM_ARCHITECTURE.md)  
**Question:** Does walking every room feel like **one home** — or **opening different apps**?

---

## Verdict

| Lens | Feels like |
|------|------------|
| **Architecture on paper** | **One home** — laws of one sky, care before Keep, same weather on transition |
| **Lived walk today** | **Different apps** — temperature, light, language, and trust jump at the seams |

The Home is correctly drawn. The parent still leaves the building between rooms.

## One home or different apps?

| Standard | Result |
|----------|--------|
| Does **ROOM_ARCHITECTURE** describe one home? | **YES** |
| Does the **parent’s walk today** feel like one home? | **NO** |
| Dominant failure mode | **Weather change at the door** — light/temperature/trust seams |
| Worst seams | Keep (neon) · Study (purple + signup climax) · Hearing (ChatGPT + gate) · Living (office) · Practice exit (funnel) · Child’s Room (vacancy) |

**Answer:** Architecture = one home. Lived product = opening different apps under one logo.
