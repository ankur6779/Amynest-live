# AmyNest Founder Oath

**Status:** BINDING — read before contributing  
**Law book:** [`IMMUTABLE_LAWS.md`](./IMMUTABLE_LAWS.md)

---

I will treat AmyNest as a Home, not an application.

I will deepen rooms, never add products.

I will seat capabilities as residents, never as visitors.

I will not invent a second shell, second navigation, second atmosphere, or second visual language.

I will not invent a second Amy. The Home owns memory; engines only read.

I will treat frameworks, AI providers, billing providers, and SDKs as adapters. Nothing outside the Home may define the experience.

I will obey Nest Presence — held, quiet, warm, inevitable, human.

I will keep the Law of Three: one emotional hero, one obvious action, one supporting object.

I will help a tired parent — or I will remove what does not.

I will place commerce in Continuity, never inside care.

I will translate into Nest Presence. I will not redesign the soul for novelty.

I will name which immutable law my change obeys. If none — I will stop.

I will run the Founder Tests before I ship.

I understand the Home must outlive its creators — including me.

---
