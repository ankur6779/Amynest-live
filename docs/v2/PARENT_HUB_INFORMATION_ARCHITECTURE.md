## Governing truth

Parents never think in modules.

They think:

| Human thought | Room answer |
|---|---|
| “I need help.” | **Help** |
| “I want to understand.” | **Understand** |
| “I need to care for them.” | **Care** |
| “I want a moment with them.” | **Together** |
| “I want to feel steadier.” | **Steady** |

Today Home already owns:

> What is the next right thing for this child, **today**?

Parent Hub must never compete with that question.  
Hub rooms **deepen, support, or care** — they do not reopen a feature catalogue.

**Room count: 5** (within 4–6).  
Each room has **one purpose**.

## 1. Room Inventory

| # | Room | One purpose | Parent enters saying… |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Help** | Get unstuck with a real question or skill worry | “I need help.” |
| 2 | **Understand** | See my child more clearly | “I want to understand.” |
| 3 | **Care** | Tend body, rhythm, feeding, health | “I need to care for them.” |
| 4 | **Together** | Share one human moment with my child | “I want to be with them.” |
| 5 | **Steady** | Regulate myself / face a hard parent moment | “I want support / confidence.” |

**Not a room:** Gaming · Command Center · tip malls · Learning Zone as six equal doors · Quick-action chip walls.

**Shell (not a room):** Active child context only.  
**Bridge (not a room):** One quiet line back to Today Home if Path needs Home’s NRT — never a second hero.

## 2. Why each room exists

### Room 1 — Help

**Why it exists:**  
When the parent is stuck, AmyNest must offer a door that feels like help — not a directory of products.

**When they enter:**  
Tanrum, sleep spiral language worry, “what do I say,” “is this normal,” speech anxiety, sudden school stress.

**Arrive feeling:** Overloaded · scared · urgent · ashamed.  
**Leave feeling:** Oriented · heard · holding one next step (or in Ask Amy / Speech with calm entry).

**Single purpose:** Unstick the parent.

---

### Room 2 — Understand

**Why it exists:**  
Parents want meaning — age, patterns, growth, identity — without being scored.

**When they enter:**  
Curious evenings, after a hard day, “who is my child becoming,” milestone wondering, reading about their stage.

**Arrive feeling:** Curious · uncertain · comparing.  
**Leave feeling:** Clearer · kinder toward the child · less alone in the pattern.

**Single purpose:** Increase true understanding (never surveillance scores).

---

### Room 3 — Care

**Why it exists:**  
Bodies need tending. For infants this is often the whole day. For older children it is food, health, rhythm — not entertainment.

**When they enter:**  
Feeding windows, sleep collapse, growth check, vaccine week, meal stress, illness aftermath.

**Arrive feeling:** Tired · responsible · practical.  
**Leave feeling:** The care act is done or planned · load lighter.

**Single purpose:** Tend the child’s physical care needs.

**Age note:** Under ~24 months this room **is** mostly Infant Care.  
After that it thins to Nutrition / Health doors — never a second Infant mall.

---

### Room 4 — Together

**Why it exists:**  
AmyNest is companionship. Sometimes the need is not “optimize” — it is “be with my child for ten minutes.”

**When they enter:**  
Weekend morning, rainy afternoon, “we have a little time,” bonding after conflict.

**Arrive feeling:** Wanting connection · low energy for “programs.”  
**Leave feeling:** Shared a moment · warm · enough.

**Single purpose:** One co-presence activity — not a creativity warehouse.

---

### Room 5 — Steady

**Why it exists:**  
The parent is also a person. Exhaustion, PTM dread, and emotional crash are not “child features.”

**When they enter:**  
After a fight, before parent-teacher meeting, 2 a.m. spiral, “I’m failing.”

**Arrive feeling:** Fragile · defensive · alone.  
**Leave feeling:** Held · slightly braver · not judged.

**Single purpose:** Steady the parent.

## 3. Emotional journey

### Hub-level film (after Today Home)

```
Today Home (decide & begin)
        ↓
Parent Hub rooms (deepen / care / steady)
        ↓
Return to life (or back to Home tomorrow)
```

Hub must feel like **the same home’s inner rooms** — not a new app.

### Per-room emotional arc

| Room | In | Through | Out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Help | Panic / stuck | One clear help path | Capable |
| Understand | Fog | Gentle clarity | Knowing |
| Care | Duty / fatigue | Practical act | Relieved |
| Together | Lonely-for-connection | Shared moment | Warm |
| Steady | Fragile | Soft support | Braver |

### Forbidden emotional exits (any room)

Score · guilt · FOMO · “you unlocked” · feature envy · dashboard overwhelm.

## 7. Cursor recommendation

### Recommend APPROVE this 5-room IA as the Parent Hub north star

| Room | Keep if Founder only keeps three |
|---|---|
| **Care** | Non-negotiable for infant AmyNest |
| **Help** | Non-negotiable for trust |
| **Steady** | Non-negotiable for exhausted parents |

**Understand** and **Together** are the fourth and fifth — they prevent Hub from becoming only crisis + logistics.

### Hard opinions

1. **Do not** recreate today’s 8 groups with nicer names. That is the mall in a new costume.  
2. **Do not** put Learning Zone back as a room. Learning is a destination; understanding is the human need.  
3. **Do not** invent a “Today” room inside Hub that competes with Today Home. That would reverse R8.  
4. **Infant does not need its own sixth room name** — under 24m, **Care expands** until it feels like the Hub.  
5. Smallest viable Hub for a first blueprint: **Help · Care · Steady**, with Understand/Together as phase-2 rooms if Founder wants thinner v1.

### Suggested Founder decision prompts

1. Approve **5 rooms** as written, or cut to **Help · Care · Steady** for v1?  
2. Does Birth Sky belong in **Understand**, or outside Parent Hub entirely?  
3. Is **Together** allowed any gamified residue, or zero?
