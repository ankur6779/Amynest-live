# AmyNest V2 — Premium Feel Audit

**Status:** Emotional craftsmanship audit only — **do not fix · no implementation · no redesign**  
**Lens:** Ignore usability. Judge how the screen *feels* as a crafted object.  
**Scale:** 1–10 per dimension. Every score below 10 carries a deduction note.  
**Baseline:** Current V2 presentation as shipped in craft (not aspirational Nest Presence mockups).  
**Companions:** Design Constitution · Optical Alignment · Spatial Rhythm · Reduction Report *(reference only — this doc does not prescribe fixes)*.

---

## Scoring rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 10 | Inevitable. Nothing to remove. Feels designed by one quiet hand. |
| 8–9 | Premium, with one or two craft leaks. |
| 6–7 | Calm intent visible; SaaS / kit habits still audible. |
| 4–5 | Utility product. Care is in copy more than materials. |
| 1–3 | Cold, loud, or generic. |

### Dimensions

| Dimension | What is judged |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Luxury** | Restraint, materials, air, refusal of decoration |
| **Warmth** | Human temperature — never clinical, never carnival |
| **Calm** | Nervous-system quiet; low visual volume |
| **Trust** | Hosted, not hustled; no trap energy |
| **Depth** | Believable light and layer — not flat navy + cards |
| **Humanity** | Feels made for a tired parent, not a growth funnel |
| **Longevity** | Could age 5 years without looking like a 2024 AI skin |
| **Timelessness** | Essential object, not trend costume |
| **Apple quality** | One object · clarity · deference · finish |
| **Japanese minimalism** | Ma · absence as structure · no surplus |
| **Scandinavian calm** | Honest light · quiet function · soft clarity |

## Hard truths (no fixes)

1. **Humanity > luxury.** Parents may feel cared for in words while still *seeing* a mid SaaS shell.  
2. **For Child hollow cards** are the single largest premium-feel fracture.  
3. **Nav chrome** caps every tabbed screen’s calm ceiling.  
4. **Ask Amy + Premium** pull timelessness toward “AI feature” and “store.”  
5. **Breath + Success + Guest sheet + Calm prepare** prove the ceiling is real — the rest hasn’t met them.  
6. **Japanese minimalism is the weakest family score** — absence is filled with eyebrows, tiles, and rules.  
7. **Depth is not lighting yet** — mostly opacity and shadows, not a room.
