# 7. Top Questions

Ask **every parent** after they finish the path.  
**Target:** under **3 minutes**. Do not turn this into an interview.

---

## Script (read aloud)

> Thanks — five quick questions. Short answers are perfect.

### Q1 — Clarity (30s)

**What was AmyNest trying to help you do today?**

Notes: _______________________________________________

### Q2 — Mission (30s)

**Did today’s activity feel doable with your child — yes, no, or not sure? Why?**

Notes: _______________________________________________

### Q3 — Friction (45s)

**Where did you hesitate or feel unsure — if anywhere?**

Notes: _______________________________________________

### Q4 — Value (45s)

**Would you pay for this? Yes / Maybe / No — and why in one sentence?**

Notes: _______________________________________________

### Q5 — Memory (30s)

**What one moment would you tell another parent about?**

Notes: _______________________________________________

---

## Optional (only if time left — 30s)

**If we improved one thing before other parents try it, what should it be?**

---

## Facilitator rules

- Do **not** explain the product during answers.  
- Do **not** defend UI. Nod and write.  
- If they rant, capture Worst moment; don’t problem-solve live.  
- Stop at 3 minutes even if incomplete — mark skipped questions.

## Scoring shortcuts (founder)

| Signal | Positive | Negative |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Q1 | Mentions child / today / speech or calm step | “Not sure” / “an AI chat app” |
| Q2 | Yes / doable | Too long / confusing / for the child to read alone |
| Q3 | None / minor | Auth wall, lost, double back, Premium surprise |
| Q4 | Yes/Maybe + concrete why | No + trust/value gap |
| Q5 | Mission complete / feeling helped | Blank / annoyance |

Transfer Yes/Maybe/No and confusion into the [Founder Dashboard](./05-FOUNDER_DASHBOARD.md).
