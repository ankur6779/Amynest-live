## Mission

Parent Hub must increase subscriptions **without becoming a sales page**.

AmyNest never sells access.  
AmyNest sells: **relief · confidence · time · consistency · understanding**.

---

## Governing laws

| Law | Monetization consequence |
|---|---|
| Home ↔ Hub Boundary | Never paywall an **action** that belongs on Today Home |
| Entry Law | Never open Hub as a shop; Hub opens for intention |
| Today Home Law | Home remains free to know & begin today’s NRT |
| Value before payment | Premium only after trust is felt |
| Gamification forbidden | Never sell points / streaks / unlock theatre |

## 1. Free vs Premium (by room)

### Help

| Always free (trust) | Premium after trust | Why pay willingly |
|---|---|---|
| Emotional Support entry (journey-exempt today) | Deeper ongoing Ask Amy beyond fair free AI | Relief in crisis — “I’m not alone” |
| PTM Prep seasonal core (journey-exempt) | Expanded prep packs / exports if ever | Confidence before a hard meeting |
| First Speech Coach sessions (today: ~3 lifetime) | Continued Speech Coach practice | Consistency for a feared skill |
| Calm “what’s hard?” orientation | — | — |

**Should never be behind a wall on first crisis visit:** Emotional Support · PTM in season · the ability to ask once.

**Premium value:** Uninterrupted help when life keeps hurting — not a locked emoji grid.

---

### Understand

| Always free (trust) | Premium after trust | Why pay willingly |
|---|---|---|
| One Guidance card that proves Amy understands this child | Full Guidance history / depth | Understanding — “finally sees my child” |
| Birth Sky entry / meaning door (launch free) | Deep AI / export (`premium_insight` today) | Identity & meaning after emotional bond |
| Quiet learning status (no XP) | Full Learning destinations continuity | Consistency of growth insight |
| First tips that change thinking | Unlimited tip/article shelf | — |

**Should never sell:** The first clear sentence about the child.  
**Premium value:** Ongoing clarity and memory of who they are becoming.

---

### Care

| Always free (trust) | Premium after trust | Why pay willingly |
|---|---|---|
| Core infant logging loops (sleep/feed/cry basics) | Advanced sleep/feeding plans (today: 1× AI then gate) | Time + relief in the hardest months |
| Essential health/vaccine reminders framing | Deep Health Lab (today: **premium-only**) | Consistency of wellness support |
| First Nutrition orientation | Nutrition AI / week plans (try-once then gate) | Time saved on meals |
| Parent wellbeing micro-check (Steady tone) | — | — |

**Highest willingness to pay:** Exhausted infant parents who need Care every day.  
**Risk today:** Health Lab premium-only can feel like a sales wall before trust — soften to “value then ask.”

---

### Moments

| Always free (trust) | Premium after trust | Why pay willingly |
|---|---|---|
| One presence offer (“ten minutes with {name}”) | Broader story/print/activity library after fair taste | Time + warmth — “we actually connect” |
| Fair sample of story/print (today: lifetime/daily caps) | Unlimited Moments library | Consistency of together-time ideas |
| Talking Amy as light presence (if kept) | — | Weak pay reason — don’t force |

**Should never sell:** The first shared moment.  
**Premium value:** Never running out of calm together ideas — not a creativity mall checkout.

## 3. Revenue review (current state)

### Current premium entry points (Hub-adjacent)

| Mechanism | How it works today | Natural or forced? |
|---|---|---|
| **Hub Journey soft-lock** | 3 free path days or 7-day calendar → soft-lock most `hub_*` | **Mixed** — natural if value earned; forced if mall browsed without value |
| **Feature-usage quotas** | Most tiles ~2 lifetime opens (journey overrides while free) | Often **forced** — feels like meter, not relief |
| **Content caps** | Story 5 / art 10 / print 2/day·6 life / speech 3 / kids-how 1 | **Natural** if after enjoyment; bad if mid-moment |
| **Entitlements** | Nutrition try-once AI; Speech try sessions; Health Lab premium-only | Health Lab = **most forced** Care gate |
| **Paywall reasons** | `hub_locked`, `hub_journey`, `hub_nutrition`, `speech_coach`, `premium_insight`, infant AI reasons | Copy varies; journey CTA can feel salesy |
| **Activation defer** | Soft paywalls defer to routine generate until first routine | Protects Home activation — **good** for Boundary Law |
| **Post-onboarding trial** | `/subscription-trial` then **Today Home** (not Hub) | Correct — don’t sell Hub before Home |
| **RevenueCat** | Native/paywall modal — Hub calls `openSubscriptionGate`, not RC directly | Preserve; don’t redesign |

### Forced premium moments (fix)

| Forced | Why bad |
|---|---|
| Soft-lock after browsing without completing Path value | Sales page energy |
| Health Lab hard premium before any Care trust | Interrupts Care intention |
| `hub_locked` on curiosity tiles with 2-open meter | Exploration → paywall = mall |
| Mid-story / mid-printable hard stop | Punishes Moments joy |
| Paywall as Hub landing | Violates Entry Law |

### Missed opportunities (opportunity — not implementation)

| Missed | Room |
|---|---|
| “Continue Help tonight” after a successful Ask Amy | Help |
| “Keep Care calm this week” after 3 infant sleep logs | Care |

| Never: “Unlock the Hub” as a product | — |

## 4. Conversion opportunities

### Subscription intent rank (highest → lowest)

| Rank | Room | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Care** | Daily necessity (esp. 0–24m). Pays for time and relief every day. Strongest habit → sub. |
| **2** | **Help** | Crisis willingness is high; converting after relief (not before) is trustworthy and sticky. |
| **3** | **Understand** | Slower burn; deep trust + Birth Sky meaning; excellent retention narrative, softer impulse buy. |
| **4** | **Moments** | Warmth and referral stories; weaker “I must pay tonight” — premium as library continuity, not urgency. |

### Room → conversion lever

| Room | Best lever | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Care | Daily Care streak of **value** (not points) → invite continuity | Premium-only Health Lab on first tap |
| Help | Post-relief invite; Speech after 3 real sessions | Lock Emotional/PTM |
| Understand | After Guidance proves right | Meter tips on day one |
| Moments | After a loved story/print | FOMO unlock copy |

### Home relationship

- **Today Home free NRT + Begin** must stay free (Boundary Law).  
- Hub monetization must never tax Home action.  
- Activation defer to first routine is aligned — keep.

## 5. Founder recommendation

### APPROVE direction (study)

1. **Monetize continuity of relief**, not access to rooms.  
2. Rank investment: **Care → Help → Understand → Moments**.  
3. Keep **Emotional + PTM** free (trust spine).  
4. Keep **Home action free** forever.  
5. Treat Journey soft-lock as acceptable **only** after Path delivered felt value — never as browse-tax.  
6. Reclassify Health Lab from “premium wall” to “premium after Care trust” in a future blueprint (not now).  
7. Moments premium = library depth after a free presence win — never gamified unlock.  
8. **No sales-page Hub landing. Ever.**
