# AmyNest — Founder Review

**Panel:** Jony Ive · Dieter Rams · Naoto Fukasawa · John Maeda  
**Mode:** Critique only — **never defend · no implementation · assume quieter is possible**  
**Subject:** AmyNest V2 as lived today (craft shell, not aspirational mockups)  
**Oath of the room:** If four masters disagree, record the disagreement. Do not average it away.

---

## How the panel thinks

| Voice | Obsession |
|-------|-----------|
| **Jony Ive** | Essentialism. If it can be removed, it is already guilty. One object. |
| **Dieter Rams** | Good design is as little design as possible. Honesty. No decorative lies. |
| **Naoto Fukasawa** | Without Thought. Soften until use disappears into life. Warm ordinary. |
| **John Maeda** | Laws of Simplicity. Emptiness as structure. More Ma. Subtract until meaning appears. |

For every screen they ask:

1. Would Jony **remove** this?  
2. Would Rams **simplify** this?  
3. Would Fukasawa **soften** this?  
4. Would Maeda create more **emptiness**?

Answer: **yes — until proven otherwise.**

## What all four agree on (no disagreement)

Recorded because silence between masters is rare — and binding:

1. **Hollow For Child cards must go.**  
2. **Nav underline + heavy shelf shadow must go.**  
3. **Eyebrow labels (“Right now,” “Speech,” “Amy Coach · Long-term,” “Today’s step”) are surplus.**  
4. **Today cannot remain a stack of equal chapters.**  
5. **One bloom. Not peer CTAs.**  
6. **“Not right now” stays as a right — even if it becomes quieter text.**  
7. **Commerce badges and selection glow are beneath the product.**  
8. **The design can always become quieter than it is today.**  
9. **Never defend the current shell.**  
10. **Copy is often ahead of craft — craft must catch up by subtraction, not addition.**
