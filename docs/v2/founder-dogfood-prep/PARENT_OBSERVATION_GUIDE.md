## Post-session prompts (optional, <3 min)

Use only if the parent is comfortable talking:

1. “What were you trying to do just now?”  
2. “Where did Amy feel most helpful?”  
3. “Where did it feel like an app instead of help?”  
4. “Would you open this again tomorrow? Why / why not?”

Do **not** coach them toward the “correct” path.
