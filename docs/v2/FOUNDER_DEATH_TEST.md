## The Test

Imagine the founder

is gone.

Can a new engineer

read only

the Home Contract

and continue AmyNest

without breaking

its soul?

If YES

the architecture survives.

If NO

rewrite the architecture.

The Home

must outlive

its creators.

---

## How to run the test

1. Hand a new engineer **only** [`V3_HOME_CONTRACT.md`](./V3_HOME_CONTRACT.md).  
2. Give a real task (engine, feature, SDK, AI provider, billing).  
3. Ask: would their work preserve Nest Presence — one Home, one Amy, rooms deepened not products added?  
4. Score YES or NO. No partial credit for tribal knowledge.

---

## Failure modes (instant NO)

- Engineer needs Founder Slack / tribal lore to know what AmyNest is  
- Engineer invents a product, second shell, or second Amy from the Contract’s silence  
- Contract points away for soul essentials (rooms, memory, API) without containing them  
- Architecture is a proposal pile, not a surviving Home  

---

## On NO

Rewrite **the Home Contract** (and only then dependent architecture) until YES.

Do not add more satellite docs as a substitute for a complete Contract.
