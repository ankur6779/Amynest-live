# R1 Image Brief — Opening Experience

**Use:** Generate Opening Experience reference images only  
**Tone:** Production art brief

---

## 1. Product promise
AmyNest helps parents know their child’s next right thing today.  
R1 does not show the answer yet. R1 opens the quiet room where that answer becomes believable.

## 2. Target parent
A tired, modern parent opening the app in noise — phone full of chaos, mind full of tabs. Adult. Discerning. Allergic to gimmicks. Needs calm competence, not entertainment.

## 3. Emotional state
Overstimulated. Guarded. Slightly hopeful. One breath away from bounce if the product feels loud.

## 4. Desired emotional outcome
Quiet relief.  
“I can breathe here.”  
“I am in the right place.”  
Willingness to continue — not pressure to convert.

## 5. Environment
A soft dark void like pre-dawn air in a quiet home. Not outer space. Not a nursery cartoon world. Not a SaaS dashboard. One still room of atmosphere.

## 6. Lighting
Low-key cinematic. Single soft key from above-front. Warm core light. Edges dissolve into deep ink. No neon. No multi-color spectacle. No lens-flare circus.

## 7. Color palette
- Deep ink / near-black stage  
- Violet dusk mid-atmosphere  
- Warm ivory light core  
- Quiet rose undertone  
- No candy primaries. No startup purple spam. No alert red.

## 8. Composition
Full-bleed vertical stage.  
Optical center column only.  
Amy living light as the sole subject, slightly above geometric midpoint.  
Vast negative space left, right, and corners.  
Nothing competing. Nothing in the edges selling.

## 9. Camera
Centered. Eye-level. Intimate still hold.  
Poster-portrait gravity. No Dutch angle. No wide chaotic establishing shot. No overhead product map.

## 10. Amy presence
Light-first companion presence — warm luminous life before mascot merchandise.  
Large enough to feel in the room; small enough that emptiness still dominates.  
Calm. Adult-premium. Not waving. Not dancing. Not sticker-cute.  
Presence says arrive.

## 11. Typography style
Almost none in the hero image.  
If type exists: one short whisper line only — soft, adult, understated, centered beneath presence after light lands.  
No hype. No exclamation. No feature headlines. No “Start free trial.”

## 12. Material language
Velvet dark field. Soft vapor. Warm inner glass glow. Silk-light haze.  
Premium stillness materials.  
No plastic UI chrome. No gloss badge candy. No glassmorphism circus.

## 13. Motion intention
(For sequence / animatic references)  
Breath-speed only: void hold → ambient inhale → Amy light rises and settles → stillness → late whisper fade.  
No bounce. No confetti. No particle storm. Exit feels like a curtain parting, not a hard cut.

## 14. Premium references
Apple product keynote stillness · Headspace arrival calm · Calm night atmospheres · Arc restrained glow · Craft soft material quiet · Airbnb emotional clarity · Linear adult focus · Mobbin premium mobile restraint

## 15. Absolutely forbidden
- Feature malls, icon grids, carousels  
- Signup / login / paywall energy  
- Kids-arcade colors and mascot spam  
- Confetti, coins, badges, trophies  
- Stock “perfect family” lifestyle collage  
- Dashboard chrome, cards, nav, buttons as heroes  
- Urgency, countdown, scarcity  
- Busy gradients, neon, cyberpunk gimmick  
- Text walls in the first open  
- Anything that makes the parent think about pricing before breath  

---

## Hero frame sentence
Deep ink air. One warm living light at center. Almost no words. No asks. Slow breath. Premium silence.

## Parent after the image
“I can breathe here. Show me this for my child.”
