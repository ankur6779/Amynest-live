# AmyNest Today — 50 Industrial Design Concepts

**Status:** Exploration only — DO NOT IMPLEMENT  
**Forget:** React · Tailwind · Components · Current UI  
**Law:** Every concept = **one emotional hero** · **one obvious action** · **one supporting object** · nothing else

---

## How to read each concept

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Feeling | What the parent’s body should feel in 1 second |
| Composition | Where hero / action / support sit in space |
| Typography | Voice of the words as material |
| Material | What the screen is “made of” |
| Lighting | How light behaves |
| Interaction | The one gesture that matters |
| Trust | Why a parent emotionally trusts it |

---

## 01 — Editorial

**Feeling:** Quiet authority — like a morning essay.  
**Composition:** Huge focus word top-left; action as a single underlined verb bottom; support as a thin caption under the word.  
**Typography:** High-contrast serif display; caption in small roman.  
**Material:** Newsprint white / ink black.  
**Lighting:** Flat daylight; no glow.  
**Interaction:** Tap the verb.  
**Trust:** Treats the parent as intelligent; no gamification.

## 02 — Museum

**Feeling:** Reverence; the day is an exhibit.  
**Composition:** Hero centered in a vast empty field; action as a small wall label button; support as a plaque with the child’s step.  
**Typography:** Museum sans, wide tracking on labels.  
**Material:** Gallery plaster, soft shadow of a frame without a frame.  
**Lighting:** Spot on the hero; periphery dim.  
**Interaction:** Approach the plaque, then tap action.  
**Trust:** Nothing shouts; care feels curated, not sold.

## 03 — Apple

**Feeling:** Inevitability — of course this is next.  
**Composition:** One product silhouette (soft plate) floating; hero type above; action engraved into the plate.  
**Typography:** Large San Francisco–like clarity; almost no secondary.  
**Material:** Milled glass-ceramic.  
**Lighting:** Soft studio key light from above-left.  
**Interaction:** Press the plate’s action region.  
**Trust:** Restraint signals competence.
