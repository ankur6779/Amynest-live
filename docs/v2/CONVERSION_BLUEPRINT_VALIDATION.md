# Conversion Blueprint Validation

**Mission:** Prove or fail [`CONVERSION_RECOVERY_BLUEPRINT.md`](./CONVERSION_RECOVERY_BLUEPRINT.md)  
**Method:** 10 first-time parents · walk proposed journey only · no implementation  
**Journey under test:**

```
Guest → First wow → Sign up to save
```

**Pass bar:** ≥ **7 / 10** naturally choose to sign up.  
**Below that:** blueprint **FAILS**. Do not fix in this document.

**Assumption for the walk:** The blueprint’s routing is followed (one worry ad → guest help → keep ask). Amy’s reply uses **existing** AI/modules only — quality is what those systems can actually produce today for that worry, not an idealized future model.

# **BLUEPRINT FAILS**

Pass bar was **7 / 10**.

---

## WHY it fails (explain only — do not fix)

1. **The blueprint optimizes sequence, not sufficiency of wow.**  
   Guest → wow → keep is the right *order*, but signup still depends on a felt “she understands *my* child.” For speech-delay and autism parents, existing general parenting AI/modules do not reliably create that feeling. Without T2 trust, Keep is rejected.

2. **“Sign up to save” assumes attachment. Some parents never attach to accounts.**  
   Grandparent and never-pay skeptic can accept a free tip and still refuse identity. The blueprint treats Keep as the natural sequel to wow; for them, wow completes the job and account is pure downside (privacy, spam, paywall foreshadow).

3. **Trust % and Signup % are the same 60% here — the miss is not “wrong ask timing,” it is “who never reaches keep-worthy trust.”**  
   Moving signup later helps the six who already get a generic-personal wow. It does not create the seventh parent from populations the current AI substrate underserves or from anti-account psychographics.

4. **Confusion remains high even when signup happens.**  
   Specialty limits, medical boundary, and “what else is this app” stay unresolved. That does not always block the six, but it shows the blueprint is a funnel reorder, not a completed trust product for all ten profiles.

5. **Therefore the blueprint is directionally correct and still fails the Founder bar.**  
   It would likely beat today’s ~10% install→signup among “general exhausted parents,” and still fails **7/10 natural signup** across this representative panel because four parents either never trust or refuse keep on principle/specialty grounds.

## STOP

Validation only. No redesign. No implementation. No fixes in this order.
