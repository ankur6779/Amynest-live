## The Law

Every new capability

must become

a better resident,

not a bigger building.

If a feature requires

its own identity,

it does not belong

inside AmyNest.

It belongs outside.

AmyNest grows

by deepening rooms,

never by adding products.

---

## Tests

| Test | Pass | Fail |
|------|------|------|
| **Better resident** | The room feels more itself with this capability | The Home feels larger, busier, or more like a suite of products |
| **No own identity** | Capability has no brand, chrome, or name that competes with Nest / the room | Feature needs its own logo, nav, splash, or product voice |
| **Deepen, don’t add** | Work lands inside an existing room’s emotional job | Work invents a product, destination, or peer surface |

---

## Outside means outside

If a capability needs its own identity, it is **not** Nested by wrapping it in Nest CSS.

It belongs **outside** AmyNest — separate product, separate Home, or not shipped here.

---

## Growth definition

| Growth | Not growth |
|--------|------------|
| Deepening an existing room | Adding a product |
| Stronger resident of Hearing / Study / Practice / … | Bigger building (new wing, new app, new identity) |
| More continuity of Home | More catalogue |
