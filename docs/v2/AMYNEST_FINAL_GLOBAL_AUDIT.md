# WHAT THE CAMPAIGN PROVED

1. The framework works for **openings** when Founder orders one module at a time.  
2. FE photography + one recommend + quiet paths can convert a storefront into a room **on the first screen**.  
3. Demotion / hiding (Talking Amy, Discovery) reduces damage but does **not** create belonging.  
4. Without manufacturing **interiors and leave-paths**, the house remains a lobby with many exits into other brands.  
5. Grow was correctly ranked high in the original audit and was **skipped** in execution order after Health Lab — Understanding still has a mall tumor.  
6. Ask Amy / Emotional — Help spine — remain unfixed while Help still leads with them as peer doors.
