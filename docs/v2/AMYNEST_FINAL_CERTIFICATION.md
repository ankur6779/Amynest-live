## The eight answers

### 1. Is the live product finally faithful to the Constitution?

**No.**

Faithful in **translated Nest rooms** on the Nest flag path (Threshold · Living · Practice · Study guest entry · Hearing entry · Continuity · Child’s): Law of Three, Nest Atmosphere, whisper exits, one Bloom — largely lived.

Unfaithful on the **path that decides trust**:
- **Keep (Signup)** still mounts purple neon / Meet AMY — Visual Constitution forbids EdTech purple and neon fog.
- **Vestibule (Front Door)** still lives as form + progress + peer Soft Plate doors — Room Architecture requires welcome, not interrogation.
- **Legacy / flag-off** Landing and pricing remain a second product.
- **Hearing thread** and **post-auth Coach** remain other-app chrome beside Nest furniture.

Constitution is law. The live Home is not yet that law end-to-end.

---

### 2. Is there any remaining visual debt?

**Yes.**

Not philosophy debt. **Lived debt:** neon Keep · Vestibule wizard meter and peer choice stacks · dual Nest/legacy costumes · Assistant chatbot Hearing chrome · post-auth Study dialect · store-sheet Continuity interrupt · residual product voice (“Build My Plan,” Speech taxonomy, lesson-like step copy, SKU period names).

### 3. If AmyNest were launching tomorrow, would you personally approve it?

**No.**

---

### 4. Would Apple reject it?

**Yes.**

**Why:** Apple stops at identity breaks. Nest porch, then neon Keep, is two products. Vestibule progress/cards read as onboarding software. Dual flag paths prove the system is unfinished. A Design Review does not grade the Living Room in isolation after the lock failed.

---

### 5. Would Headspace reject it?

**Yes.**

**Why:** Headspace requires continuous hold. The path raises shoulders at Signup (anxiety theatre) and at Vestibule interrogation. Hearing opens into chatbot chrome at the vulnerable moment. Calm Nest rooms after a costume change do not restore the body that already braced.

---

### 6. Would Airbnb reject it?

**Yes.**

**Why:** Hospitality is one welcome under one roof. Vestibule does not yet feel “received.” Keep does not feel “your place is held.” Guest → neon portal is a host who changes the house mid-check-in. Translated Living Room hospitality cannot redeem a broken arrival.

---

### 7. Would Linear reject it?

**Mostly yes — with a narrower reason.**

**Why:** Linear rejects manufacturing inconsistency. Nest rooms now show real hierarchy; Keep, legacy Landing, Hearing thread, and post-auth Coach prove multiple systems still ship as one app. Hierarchy wins in places. System unity does not.
