2. **Parenting Situation** — Points and badges blur together. Nothing feels collectible — so nothing feels earned.
3. **Problem** — Random points feel empty — kids forget why they earned them.
4. **Emotion** — Pride sticks when a sticker represents real listening and learning.

### Show, Don’t Tell

- Prefer faces, hands, props, and environment over explanatory VO.
- If a line can be removed and the shot still reads, remove the line.
- Product UI appears as a consequence of the feeling — never as the cold open.
- Last 5 seconds: show the before→after contrast in one glance.
