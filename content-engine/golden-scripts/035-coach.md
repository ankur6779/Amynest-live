## Muted Video Test (Visual Story First)

**PASS required.** Design as a visual story first and a narrated story second. Prefer showing over telling.

### First 10 Seconds — Sound OFF

Muted 0–10s: parent on bed edge / phone face-down + faces tell the emotional problem before any app appears.

| Window | SHOW (no audio needed) | MUTED VIEWER READS |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Wide/intimate domestic frame: parent on bed edge / phone face-down in focus; parent’s posture already tired; Amy AI not yet the hero — the room is. | A real parenting night is starting — and it’s heavy. |
| 3–7s | Close-up chain: child’s eyes / hands / parent on bed edge / phone face-down — the situation (“It’s late. The question is small and urgent. Search results are…”) plays as pictures, not speech. | We understand the struggle without hearing a word. |
| 7–10s | Parent and child miss each other by inches — a look, a turned shoulder, a paused hand. Emotion peaks. Still no product UI. | The muted viewer feels the problem in their chest. |

### Last 5 Seconds — No Narration

Muted last 5s: visual before→after + Ask Amy Assistant proof, then end card.

| Window | SHOW (no narration needed) | MUTED VIEWER READS |
|---|---|---|
| T−5–T−2s | Amy AI in frame: parent’s posture opens; a clear next-step card appears in hand/on screen. Soft purple light; faces soften; the earlier prop reappears transformed. | Something helped — you can see the change without narration. |
| T−2–T−0s | Hold the hope image (“A steady voice in the dark can change the whole night.”), then gentle settle into official AmyNest end card (icon + store badges) — hope first, download second. | AmyNest is why the evening got lighter — shown, not lectured. |

### Silent Story Beats

- Silent prop: parent on bed edge / phone face-down

### Show, Don’t Tell

- Prefer faces, hands, props, and environment over explanatory VO.
- If a line can be removed and the shot still reads, remove the line.
- Product UI appears as a consequence of the feeling — never as the cold open.
- Last 5 seconds: show the before→after contrast in one glance.
