# CHARACTER_PERFORMANCE_GUIDE

**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Brand:** AmyNest AI  
**Purpose:** Permanent performance bible for the three official marketing mascots  

Every marketing Short is a **new episode** starring the same cast — not a new cast of AI strangers.

---

## 1. The permanent cast

| Character | Role | Appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Amy AI** | Host · narrator · guide | **Every** video |
| **Amy Girl** | Main child learner | Learning / speech / health / routine / creativity episodes |
| **Amy Boy** | Main child learner | Math / logic / science / games / adventure episodes |

**Hard rule:** Never replace these three with random children, parents-as-heroes, or redesigned mascots. Parents may appear as **supporting environment** only when needed for emotional context — they are never the brand face.

## 3. Performance roles

### 3.1 Amy AI — always the host

Amy AI **performs**. She does not decorate.

| Beat | Performance |
|---|---|
| Cold open handoff | Floats into frame after the hook |
| Welcome | Looks to camera, soft smile, open gesture |
| Explain | Points / nods toward the learning problem or tablet |
| Guide transition | Turns body toward next subject, eye-line leads the cut |
| Feature assist | Points at progress ring / lesson card **inside a device in-world** |
| Celebrate | Small bounce / glow pulse when child succeeds |
| CTA invite | Waves toward camera / store badges; inviting, never salesy-hard |

**Signature motions**

- Float in / float out (gentle arc, not teleport)  
- Wave  
- Point (hand/arm clear silhouette)  
- Look-to-camera ↔ look-to-child  
- Blink + micro head tilt  
- Halo brightness reacts to emotion (soft, not rave neon)

**Never**

- Full-screen sticker  
- Static bible sheet  
- Mute floating watermark  
- Talking without eye/hand life  

### 3.2 Amy Girl — main learner performances

| Beat | Performance |
|---|---|
| Struggle (optional) | Slumps at desk, glances at unfinished worksheet, soft frown — never distress/horror |
| Discovery | Eyes widen; turns toward Amy AI or tablet |
| Interaction | Opens tablet / taps lesson / holds phone with two hands |
| Focus | Tongue-of-concentration micro expression OK; keeps hoodie lock |
| Success | Smile, small clap, or hug of tablet |
| With Amy AI | Shared eye contact; Amy AI midground guide, Girl foreground learner |

**Signature motions**

- Walk into study nook  
- Sit → lean in  
- Point at screen  
- Tap / swipe (clear finger travel)  
- Smile to camera for hope beat  
- Look up to Amy AI for reassurance  

### 3.3 Amy Boy — main learner performances

| Beat | Performance |
|---|---|
| Challenge | Furrowed brow at puzzle / math card |
| Attempt | Taps abacus / lesson; body leans forward |
| Breakthrough | Eyes light; fist pump or jump (short, safe) |
| Celebrate | Arms up, big smile — lesson complete |
| With Amy AI | Amy AI points; Boy follows the cue |

**Signature motions**

- Walk / jog-in (light)  
- Jump (small)  
- Point  
- Celebrate  
- High-five gesture toward Amy AI (optional)

## 4. Episode casting rules

| Episode pillar | Primary child | Amy AI |
|---|---|---|
| Learning / speech / health / routine / creativity | Amy Girl | Required host |
| Math / logic / science / games / adventure / astro | Amy Boy | Required host |
| Parenting / coach / premium / general brand | Either child supporting, or Amy AI solo with device prop | Required host |

**Both children** may appear in family hope beats, but one remains the clear learner focus.

---

## 5. Scene performance grammar (continuous shots)

Every shot must feel like a Pixar / DreamWorks / Duolingo mascot beat — **not** a slide.

### Required life in every shot

| Layer | Must include |
|---|---|
| Camera | Push, pan, dolly, or slow orbit — continuous |
| Body | Walk, float, turn, sit, lean, or celebrate |
| Face | Blink, look, smile, or talk mouth motion |
| Hands | Wave, point, tap, hold device, clap |
| Interaction | Character ↔ character, or character ↔ device |
| Depth | Foreground / midground / background + motivated light |

### Banned shot types

- Raw PNG / bible fullscreen  
- Static Ken Burns on a still as the *only* motion  
- Random AI children replacing Amy Girl / Amy Boy  
- Fullscreen app screenshot as the world  
- PowerPoint text cards as story  

---

## 6. App presentation (story prop, not the story)

| Rule | Spec |
|---|---|
| Max on-screen | **≤ 2 seconds** as readable UI focus |
| Framing | Inside phone, tablet, desk mount, or held device |
| Interaction | Child or Amy AI must *use* the device (open, tap, point) |
| Content | Real AmyNest feature moment (Study Zone, progress ring, lesson card) |
| Never | Fullscreen screenshot montage; CTA screenshots as “UI” |

---

## 7. Shot recipe for a ~20s episode

| # | Duration | Cast | Performance intent |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 2–3s | Environment (+ optional parent silhouette) | Cinematic hook — unfinished worksheets / boredom |
| 2 | 3–4s | **Amy AI** | Floats in, welcomes parents, names the feeling |
| 3 | 4–5s | **Amy Girl or Amy Boy** + Amy AI | Child interacts with tablet; Amy AI guides |
| 4 | ≤2s | Device + hand / Amy AI point | App progress moment inside frame |
| 5 | 3–4s | Child (+ Amy AI) | Hope / celebrate / calm confidence |
| 6 | 3–4s | **Amy AI** + brand CTA | Wave invite; Download AmyNest AI |

Total ~20s. Each row is a **continuous generated performance**, not a still.

## 8. Prompting language for performances

Use **motion-first** language. When a reference/first frame already locks identity, do **not** re-describe wardrobe (prevents drift).

### Good (performance)

> Same Amy AI as reference. She floats into a warm living room from frame-left, waves to camera, then points to a tablet on the coffee table. Slow push-in, soft purple rim light, Pixar family lighting. Gentle smile, blinks once.

### Bad (redesign / montage)

> Cute purple robot girl, new design, standing still, poster background, text overlay, UI screenshot fullscreen.

### Character call sheets (copy blocks)

**Amy AI**

> Official Amy AI only: white rounded soft-polymer floating body, purple AmyAI cap with headphones, large purple eyes, soft purple halo. Perform: [WAVE / POINT / FLOAT-IN / INVITE]. Never redesign.

**Amy Girl**

> Official Amy Girl only: brown side ponytail with yellow bow, plain purple hoodie, dark purple leggings, purple sneakers with white soles, large brown eyes. Perform: [OPEN TABLET / TAP / SMILE / LOOK TO AMY AI]. Never redesign.

**Amy Boy**

> Official Amy Boy only: fluffy dark brown hair, plain purple hoodie, dark purple joggers, purple sneakers with white soles, warm brown eyes. Perform: [TRY LESSON / CELEBRATE / JUMP / POINT]. Never redesign.

## 9. Interaction patterns (cast chemistry)

| Pairing | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Amy AI → Parent (camera) | Host eye-line to lens; warm invitation |
| Amy AI → Child | Guide stance slightly above/beside; points; child follows |
| Child → Device | Hands on device; face lit by screen; genuine micro-reactions |
| Child → Success | Celebrate toward Amy AI, then soft look to camera |
| Family hope | Child + Amy AI midground; home depth behind; no random hero kids |

## 10. Emotional tone

AmyNest tone = **warm · encouraging · family-safe · hopeful**.

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---|---|
| Mild frustration → relief | Fear, shame, crying close-ups |
| Quiet pride | Hyperactive chaos |
| Soft wonder | Horror lighting, glitch terror |
| Gentle humor | Mocking parents or children |

---

## 11. Continuity across the channel

Think **series**, not one-offs:

- Same three faces every upload  
- Same outfits every episode  
- Same purple lighting language (`#6A2CFF` / `#461EA8` accents)  
- Same host behavior from Amy AI  
- New *story problem* each episode — not new characters  

If a viewer mutes the video, they should still recognize: **“That’s AmyNest — Amy AI and the kids again.”**

---

## 12. QA gates before publish (creative)

Fail the cut if any are true:

1. A random child appears as the learner  
2. Amy AI looks like a different robot  
3. Hair/clothes changed across shots  
4. Any hero shot is clearly a still plate with only crop-zoom  
5. App UI dominates >2s or goes fullscreen without device frame  
6. Amy AI is absent from a video that is not an explicit brand exception  
7. Final CTA lacks Amy AI invite performance
