## Decision rule (do not skip)

2. If the portal or the current Rules require Form 28 to obtain that scale → complete the official form from verified identity documents.
3. If a company, startup, or MSME is to become the applicant → Form 28 (and assignment) **cannot** be drafted from this record. Eligibility documents do not exist here.
